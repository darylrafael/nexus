### 1. Indonesian Economic News: BI Holds BI-Rate, Raises Deposit Facility Amidst Rupiah Pressure
- **Summary**: Bank Indonesia (BI) maintained its benchmark BI-Rate at 5.75% following the October 20-21, 2026, Board of Governors Meeting. Despite these efforts, the Rupiah continued to weaken, closing at Rp18,060 per US Dollar on Thursday, October 22, 2026 (src_40149fdbc5). BI projects global economic growth at 3% for 2026 with persistent high inflation, while also forecasting new credit growth in Q4 2026 (src_a4db0b4237).
- **Market Impact**: Neutral to Slightly Bullish | **Confidence**: 55%
- **Rationale**: The decision to hold the BI-Rate is generally neutral for the market, preventing a direct tightening shock. However, the increase in the Deposit Facility rate could subtly tighten banking liquidity, potentially impacting bank margins. Despite Rupiah weakness, the banking sector, particularly state-owned banks, showed resilience and supported the IHSG on the previous day. The projection for new credit growth in Q4 2026 offers a positive outlook for the financial sector.
