### 1. Indonesian Economic News: Rupiah Terus Melemah di Tengah Inflasi Pangan dan Geopolitik
-   **Summary**: The Rupiah continued its weakening trend against the US Dollar, closing at Rp18.156 per USD on November 2nd (Bloomberg) and further weakening to Rp18.160 per USD in the spot market on November 3rd, representing a significant depreciation (src_38d0777b14, src_eed23a34fa). This depreciation is attributed to escalating geopolitical conflicts in the Middle East, continued monetary tightening in the US, and negative global investor sentiment towards emerging markets (src_81a4893bb8). Bank Indonesia (BI) has prepared four measures to stabilize the currency, including market intervention and adjusting GWM (src_81a4893bb8).
-   **Market Impact**: Bearish | **Confidence**: 70%
-   **Rationale**: A persistently weakening Rupiah increases import costs for companies, potentially compressing margins, and raises the burden of USD-denominated debt. This creates a negative sentiment for the broader market, especially for sectors reliant on imported raw materials or with significant foreign currency debt. The BI's intervention measures might provide some short-term relief but the underlying pressures remain.
-   **Affected Stocks**:
    *   CPIN (-2.35% on Nov 2): As a consumer goods manufacturer, higher import costs for raw materials due to Rupiah depreciation are bearish (src_53f237d760).
