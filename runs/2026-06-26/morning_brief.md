### 1. Indonesian Economic News: **BI‑Rate hike to 5,75 % (18‑19 Jun 2026)**  
- **Summary**: Bank Indonesia raised the policy rate by 25 bps to 5,75 % on 18‑19 Jun 2026, citing the need to curb inflation that was 3,08 % YoY in May and to stabilise the rupiah. The move also lifted the Deposit Facility to 4,75 % and the Lending Facility to 6,50 %.  
- **Market Impact**: **Bearish** | **Confidence**: **85 %**  
- **Rationale**: Higher rates increase funding costs for corporates and dampen consumer credit, pressuring sectors that are rate‑sensitive (property, consumer goods, automotive). The index opened lower on 26 Jun, reflecting the drag from tighter monetary policy.  
- **Affected Stocks**:  
  * **TLKM** – down 7,19 % on 25 Jun (source src_b135acadb2); higher financing costs hurt telecom capex.  
  * **BRPT** – down 5,42 % on 25 Jun (source src_44ee32db07); higher borrowing costs compress upstream margins.

### 2. Global Macroeconomic Updates: **U.S. Fed holds rates at 3.5‑3.75 % (17 Jun 2026)**  
- **Summary**: The Federal Reserve kept the federal funds rate unchanged at 3.5‑3.75 % on 17 Jun 2026, while projecting U.S. inflation (PCE) at 3,6 % and GDP growth at 2,2 %. The decision signalled a “higher‑for‑longer” stance, keeping global risk‑off sentiment elevated.
